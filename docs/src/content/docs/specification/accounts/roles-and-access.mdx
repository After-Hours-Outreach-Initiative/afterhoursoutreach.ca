## Account roles

| Role      | Avatar and badge                          | Access                                                  |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Volunteer | <RoleAppearance />                        | Manages their own profile and books eligible events.    |
| Organizer | <RoleAppearance badges={["organizer"]} /> | Reviews applications and manages volunteers and events. |

Event access also depends on account status and approvals. Organizers keep the
avatar for their volunteer category.

## Volunteer categories

| Category           | Avatar and badges                                                                         | Meaning                                           |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| New volunteer      | <RoleAppearance category="new-volunteer" badges={["new-volunteer"]} />                    | Awaiting patrol approval.                         |
| Outreach volunteer | <RoleAppearance />                                                                        | Patrol-approved volunteer outside the medic team. |
| Medic              | <RoleAppearance category="medic" badges={["medic"]} />                                    | Patrol-approved volunteer on the medic team.      |
| New medic          | <RoleAppearance category="new-volunteer" medicMark badges={["new-volunteer", "medic"]} /> | Medic-team volunteer awaiting patrol approval.    |

## Role changes

New accounts start as Volunteers with <RoleAppearance category="new-volunteer" badges={["new-volunteer"]} />.
